The ecosystem connects five dedicated portals in real time using Supabase PostgreSQL webhooks and stateful triggers:

📱 Customer Ordering & Buffet Portal

  • Dynamic branch selection with live distance computation
  • Instant Dine-in Table QR token auto-routing
  • Home Delivery with automatic per-kilometer delivery fee calculations
  • Open Buffet pass registration with digital verification QR ticket
  • Live customer order tracking with status updates and WhatsApp support

🏢 Branch Admin Operations Portal

  • Live POS order management & order status transition pipeline
  • Branch capability toggles (Dine-in, Takeaway, Delivery on/off)
  • Dynamic delivery zone builder & pricing rules per branch
  • One-click printable QR table standee cards for customer tables
  • Branch-specific menu inventory management and availability flags

👨‍🍳 Kitchen Display System (KDS)

  • Real-time bump bar with acoustic chime notifications on new orders
  • Itemized kitchen prep checklist with variant details & customer notes
  • Elapsed preparation timers with urgency color coding
  • Instant status dispatch (PENDING ➔ PREPARING ➔ READY)

🛵 Rider & Delivery Dispatch Portal

  • Real-time ready delivery order queue
  • One-click WhatsApp customer dispatch slip generation
  • Direct Google Maps turn-by-turn navigation deep-links
  • Cash on Delivery collection tracking and rider delivery completion

👑 Executive Owner Financial & Audit Portal

Designed exclusively for restaurant owners and general partners to maintain absolute fiscal control:

  • Multi-branch consolidated vs. branch-specific comparative revenue analytics

4. End-to-End Operational Guidance & Test Walkthrough

To experience the full multi-portal workflow as a customer, kitchen chef, delivery rider, and owner:

Step A: Place a Customer Order (Dine-in / Delivery)

1. Open ok-restuerent.vercel.app or click the Table 1 QR Link.
2. Select items (e.g., Mutton Karahi, Zinger Burger, Cold Drinks) and proceed to Checkout.
3. Confirm with Cash on Delivery / Pay at Counter. Note down your Order ID (e.g., #OK-1002).

Step B: Admin Confirmation & Kitchen Real-Time Cook

1. Open /admin in a new window and confirm the incoming order.
2. Open /kitchen to see the order appear with an audio chime.
3. Click "Start Preparing" and then "Mark as Ready" when food is prepared.

Step C: Rider Dispatch & Delivery Handover

1. Open /rider. The ready delivery order will be visible.
2. Click "Share via WhatsApp" to auto-populate the customer delivery receipt slip.
3. Click "Deliver Order" to record cash collected and close out the order.

Step D: Owner Sales Ledger & PDF Export

1. Open /owner.
2. Observe that total sales, daily ledger, and hourly trends update instantly.
3. Click "Export / Print Financial PDF" to view the certified high-contrast A4 executive audit statement.
